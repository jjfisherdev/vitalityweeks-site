# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: February 24, 2026**

This privacy policy describes how Vitality Weeks ("the App"), developed by JJ Fisher ("we," "us," or "our"), handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## Data Storage

All data you enter into Vitality Weeks is stored locally on your device. This includes your birthdate, milestones, notification preferences, and theme settings. Your data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

## Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data whatsoever. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no telemetry, and no third-party tracking of any kind. We have no servers and no databases. We cannot access your data.

## Notifications

The App uses local notifications only. Notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device by the iOS operating system. No data is sent to any external server to schedule or deliver notifications.

## Third-Party Content

The App may contain links to third-party websites such as Wikipedia for informational purposes. We do not control, endorse, or assume responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party websites. Accessing third-party links is at your own risk.

## Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on the security of your device. We recommend using a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID to protect your device. We are not responsible for unauthorized access to your device or any data stored on it.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 or any other age group. Since the App collects no data from any user, it is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

## Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all App data at any time by deleting the App from your device. We retain no data on any server.
