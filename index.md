# Vitality Weeks

**Your life, one week at a time.**

Vitality Weeks is an iOS app that shows your life as a grid of weeks. Track personal milestones, see exactly where you are right now, and get a daily fact about someone who achieved something remarkable at your age.

## Features

- Life-in-weeks Home Screen widget
- Daily age-based facts from history
- Personal milestone tracker
- Available in English, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese, and Korean
- Works fully offline
- No ads, no accounts, no tracking
